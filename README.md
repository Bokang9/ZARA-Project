## 3. Project Overview

The ZARA South Africa Website Redesign is a proposed website project aimed at creating a modern, responsive and user-friendly online experience for ZARA customers in South Africa.

ZARA is one of the world's foremost international fashion retailers and operates under the Inditex Group. Founded in Spain in 1975 by Amancio Ortega and Rosalia Mera, ZARA has expanded to more than 90 countries worldwide, including South Africa. ZARA offers customers a wide selection of clothing, footwear, accessories and beauty products for women, men and children.

The redesigned website aims to maintain ZARA's clean, modern and minimalist brand identity while improving the way users find products, access customer support and locate physical stores. The website will be developed using HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript, with responsive web design to support both desktop and mobile users.

The project will include a Home Page, About Us Page, Product Page, Store Locator and Customer Support Page. The website will focus on simple navigation, clear information, large product imagery and an improved overall user experience.

## 4. Website Goals and Objectives

### Goals

* Increase online clothing sales.
* Improve the online shopping experience.
* Increase customer engagement.
* Promote new fashion collections.
* Make products easier for customers to find and explore.
* Improve access to customer support.
* Help customers locate physical ZARA stores.

### Objectives

* Provide clear and simple navigation.
* Allow users to browse products in an organised product grid.
* Allow users to filter and sort products.
* Provide wishlist functionality.
* Provide customers with useful support and FAQ information.
* Provide a store locator for South African customers.
* Provide a responsive experience across different screen sizes.
* Improve accessibility and usability.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Increase website traffic by 25%.
* Improve online sales conversions.
* Reduce cart abandonment rates.
* Increase registered customer accounts.
* Improve customer satisfaction ratings.

## 5. Key Features and Functionality

### Home Page

* Promotional banner.
* Navigation menu.
* New arrivals and promotional content.
* Newsletter signup.
* Shop Now button.

**Functionality:** Users can view promotions and new arrivals, navigate to different sections of the website and subscribe to the newsletter.

### About Us Page

* ZARA company information.
* Company history.
* Mission and brand information.
* Careers section.
* View Careers button.

**Functionality:** Users can learn about ZARA's history, brand and global presence and access career information.

### Product Page

* Product grid.
* Product categories.
* Product filtering.
* Product sorting.
* Wishlist functionality.
* Pagination.

**Functionality:** Users can browse products, filter products by Women, Men, Kids, New Arrivals and Sale, sort products by price or popularity and add products to a wishlist.

### Store Locator

* Store search.
* Store list.
* Store addresses.
* Distance information.
* Interactive map.

**Functionality:** Users can enter a city or postcode to find ZARA stores and view their locations.

### Customer Support Page

* FAQs.
* Help topics.
* Customer reviews.
* Customer ratings.
* Contact form.

**Functionality:** Users can find answers to common questions, view customer feedback and send an enquiry using the contact form.

### Responsive Design

The website will use responsive web design so that the layout adapts to desktop, tablet and mobile screen sizes.
