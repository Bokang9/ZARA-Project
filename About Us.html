About ZARA

ZARA is one of the world's foremost international fashion retailers and operates under the Inditex Group. Founded in Spain in 1975 by Amancio Ortega and Rosalia Mera, ZARA has expanded to more than 90 countries worldwide, including South Africa.

In South Africa, ZARA offers customers an extensive selection of clothing, footwear, accessories, and beauty products for women, men, and children. The brand is distinguished by its ability to introduce the latest fashion trends to the market rapidly through its retail stores and e-commerce platform.

The ZARA South Africa website enables convenient online shopping, secure payment methods, delivery services, and product ranges adapted to the local market.
